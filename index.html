Privacy Policy for Nur AI Quran

Last updated: October 29, 2025

Introduction

Nur AI Quran ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Nur AI Quran is designed with privacy in mind:

  • We DO NOT collect personal information
  • We DO NOT track your reading habits or behavior
  • We DO NOT sell or share your data with third parties
  • We DO NOT require account creation or login

Data Storage

All user-generated content is stored locally on your device:

  • Bookmarked verses are saved only on your device
  • Reading progress is tracked locally
  • Study notes remain private on your device
  • No user data is transmitted to our servers or any third party
  • If you delete the app, all local data is permanently removed

Third-Party Services

Our app may use the following third-party services:

  • Quran API Services: To fetch authentic Quranic verses and translations. These services may have their own privacy policies.
  • AI Services: For providing explanations and answering questions about Islamic teachings. No personal information is shared with these services.

Children's Privacy

Our app is suitable for users of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect any information from users, including children under 13.

Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data depends on your device's security measures. We recommend:

  • Using device passcode/biometric protection
  • Keeping your device's operating system up to date
  • Using official app stores to download the app

Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: All your data is accessible within the app
  • Delete: You can clear all data by deleting the app
  • Export: You can view all bookmarked verses and notes within the app

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

International Users

Our app is available worldwide. Since no data is collected or transmitted, there are no international data transfer concerns.
